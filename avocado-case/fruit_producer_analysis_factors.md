# Fruit Producer Data Analysis Factors (4, 7, 10)

### 4. Channel Performance
Compares margins, volume, and reliability across all sales outlets (wholesale, direct-to-consumer, institutional buyers, farm stands). For single-fruit producers, some channels have hidden costs (e.g., DTC requires packaging/labor) that erase apparent gains.
**Typical methods**:
- Allocate all direct/indirect costs to each channel (e.g., DTC: website fees, shipping; wholesale: distributor commissions, bulk packaging)
- Calculate net margin per channel: `(Channel revenue - Channel-specific costs) / Channel revenue`
- Track month-over-month volume variance to identify stable vs volatile outlets
- Survey channel partners for pain points (e.g., inconsistent sizing, late deliveries) to improve retention

### 7. Market Price Trends
Tracks seasonal/annual price fluctuations for your specific fruit to time sales and maximize revenue. Commodity fruit prices can swing 30-50% between peak and off-peak periods.
**Typical methods**:
- Subscribe to regional commodity price reports (e.g., USDA Agricultural Marketing Service) for weekly wholesale/retail benchmarks
- Scrape terminal market price data or use agricultural data APIs to build a historical price dataset
- Use basic time series analysis (seasonal decomposition, moving averages) to identify recurring price patterns
- Monitor supply shocks (weather, pest outbreaks, trade changes) in competing regions that drive price spikes
- If you have cold storage, model storage costs vs expected off-season price premiums

### 10. Competitor Differentiation
Assesses direct regional competitors (same fruit, same growing area) to identify gaps in certifications, practices, or offerings to stand out and avoid price wars.
**Typical methods**:
- Audit top 5-10 competitors: document certifications (organic, Fair Trade), growing practices, packaging, pricing, delivery terms
- Survey key buyers (wholesalers, retailers) to rank valued traits (e.g., consistent sizing vs organic status)
- Run a SWOT analysis comparing your operation to competitors (e.g., calculate cost of organic certification vs expected price premium if no competitors are certified)
- Track competitor promotional activity to adjust your positioning (e.g., lean into premium quality messaging instead of matching price cuts)
